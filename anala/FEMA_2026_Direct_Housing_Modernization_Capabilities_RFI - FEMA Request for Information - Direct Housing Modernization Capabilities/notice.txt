 The Department of Homeland Security, Federal Emergency Management Agency, Office of Direct Housing Operations, is conducting market research to identify and seek capabilities from industry in five primary functional areas: 

   Automated Real Estate Aggregation and Vacancy Sourcing   

 - Automated discovery and ingestion of residential rental property listings via Multiple Listing Services (MLS), property management software APIs, commercial rental boards, and direct landlord entry portals. 

 - Real-time vacancy validation to prevent matching against obsolete or unavailable listings. 

 - Rapid geo-fencing within disaster-declared zones and surrounding reasonable-commute perimeters. 

   Artificial Intelligence and Algorithmic Matching   

 - Multi-constraint matching algorithms that pair applicant household profiles with available properties in seconds. 

 - Multi-point geospatial proximity optimization factoring in school district retention, work commutes, medical/provider travel distance, and essential services. 

 - Household composition sizing aligned with HUD Housing Quality Standards (HQS) and FEMA occupancy guidelines. 

   Automated Physical Accessibility Verification   

 - Algorithmic filtering and computer vision inspection tools capable of evaluating floor plans, entry photos, and property specifications against UFAS and ADA physical accessibility standards. 

 - Rapid identification of barrier-free environments, ramps, ground-floor units, grab bars, and wheelchair-accessible kitchens and bathrooms. 

   Landlord Engagement and Leasing Workflow Automation   

 - Automated outreach tools and self-service portals for landlords to submit properties, accept lease terms, and coordinate inspections. 

 - Integration with standard government lease templates and automated electronic signature workflows. 

   Secure Federal Architecture   

 - Enterprise data security architecture compliant with the Federal Information Security Modernization Act (FISMA) at the Moderate impact baseline. 

 - FedRAMP-authorized cloud hosting (or capability to achieve FedRAMP Moderate/High authorization). 

 - Strict data protection for Sensitive Personally Identifiable Information (PII) belonging to disaster survivors.
